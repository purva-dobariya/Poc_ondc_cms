    Building Blocks Behind the Network

    Digital commerce creates the greatest value when opportunity is accessible to every business. From neighbourhood kiranas to national brands, ONDC provides shared infrastructure that helps participants connect with customers, reduce costs and grow across an open network.

    01
    Building block

    DigiCatalog

    Create once. Sell across many channels.

    Build a single product catalogue for multiple commerce destinations with AI-powered infrastructure for discoverability, compliance, validation, and export readiness.

    Unified catalogue
    02
    Building block

    Gateway

    One connection. Thousands of opportunities.

    Access buyers, sellers, logistics providers, and service partners through a common network layer, helping businesses expand reach without managing multiple one-to-one integrations.

    One connection, many partners
    03
    Building block

    Registry

    Find trusted partners faster

    A verified network directory that helps participants discover, trust, and connect with each other while reducing onboarding friction and improving interoperability.

    Verified network directory
    04
    Building block

    Discovery & Search

    Reach customers beyond your own storefront

    Help products and services get discovered across a growing network of buyer applications, increasing visibility without raising customer acquisition costs.

    Beyond your own storefront
    05
    Building block

    Reconciliation & Payments

    Spend less time matching transactions

    Simplify settlements and payment tracking across multiple network participants to improve operational efficiency as businesses scale.

    Real-time settlement
    06
    Building block

    Dispute Resolution

    Trust backed by accountability

    Resolve issues faster through structured grievance and dispute frameworks that build confidence and transparency across every transaction.

    Structured resolution
    07
    Building block

    Shared Commerce Services

    Enterprise-grade capabilities without enterprise costs

    Access common commerce services that reduce the need to build and maintain complex infrastructure. Helping businesses launch faster while lowering technology investments.

    Launch faster, together
    08
    Building blockComing soon

    Verifiable Credentials

    Trust that is proven, not assumed.

    A two-layer trust system that gives every seller and network participant a verified identity, and makes credentials, government licences, business registrations, quality scores and reputation visible, verified and portable across the entire network.

    Verified, portable trust
    09
    Building blockComing soon

    Bharat Tourism Stack

    Verified Indian experiences, discoverable at last.

    Built with the Ministry of Tourism: a trust, verification, onboarding and transacting layer that verifies tourist and provider identity and surfaces credentials, licences and certifications at the point of booking — opening up supply that has stayed largely offline to date.

    Verified at the point of booking
